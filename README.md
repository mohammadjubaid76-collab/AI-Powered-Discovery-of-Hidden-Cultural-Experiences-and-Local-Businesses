# AI-Powered-Discovery-of-Hidden-Cultural-Experiences-and-Local-Businesses
An AI-powered platform to discover hidden cultural experiences, local artists, and small businesses in Gwalior.


## Project Overview
An AI-powered platform designed to help visitors discover hidden cultural experiences, local artists, and small businesses in Gwalior.

## Objective
To improve the discoverability of local cultural experiences and businesses through AI-assisted recommendations and time-aware discovery.

## Proposed Features
- Discover lesser-known cultural places and experiences.
- Explore local artists and small businesses.
- Get recommendations based on available time.
- Support visitors in planning their exploration.
